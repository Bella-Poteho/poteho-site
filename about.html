Our story

Built by people who actually love pets

Poteho was founded in 2020 with one simple idea: pet products should be safe, durable and nice to look at. In just a few years we've grown into a trusted partner for brands and retailers across the world.

We're still picky about materials, and we still treat every order — whether it's 100 units or 100,000 — like it matters. Because it does.
