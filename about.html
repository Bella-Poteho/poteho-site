Our story

Built by people who actually love pets

Poteho started on a factory floor in 2014 with one simple idea: pet products should be safe, durable and nice to look at. Over the years we've grown into a full OEM/ODM partner for brands and retailers across the world.

We're still picky about materials, and we still treat every order — whether it's 100 units or 100,000 — like it matters. Because it does.
