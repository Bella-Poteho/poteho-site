Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy explains how Poteho ("we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They let a site remember your actions and preferences over time, so you don't have to re-enter them on every visit.

How we use cookies

  • Essential: required for core functions such as navigation and security. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics: help us understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, approximate region) so we can improve it. Data is aggregated and anonymous.
  • Preferences: remember choices like your cookie consent, so we don't ask again.

Types we use

  • Session cookies — deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies — remain for a set period to remember your preferences.

Managing your choices

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies via the banner when you first visit. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

Third parties

We may use trusted third-party services (such as analytics providers) that set their own cookies, governed by their respective privacy policies.
